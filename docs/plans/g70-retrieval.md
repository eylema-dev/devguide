## Review Focus

1. An equal-looking reopened/foreign ProjectStore must not let a retained handle/session reuse private chunks; denial precedes private status (Task3).
2. A boundary-cut UTF8 scalar or lowercase expansion must not skip source bytes or silently merge incompatible lexical semantics (Task1).
3. A valid checksum with invalid ranges/terms/vector binding, or a corrupt trailing generation, must not fall back to earlier content (Task2).
4. Expiry/revocation during result staging or index synchronization must return no partial result/acknowledgement; uncertain writes require reopen (Task3).
5. Equal cutoff scores, low response/resource caps and a small authorized allowlist must not disclose unrequested sources or silently pick one tie winner (Tasks1/3).
